04 / Before touching a fuseREAD FIRST

Protect the circuit—and yourself.

The owner guide says a repeatedly blown fuse indicates a circuit problem that must be corrected. A fuse is a protective device, not the root cause.

  • Switch the ignition and affected loads off before inspection.
  • Use only the specified fuse type and amperage. Never substitute a higher rating, foil, wire, or a bridge.
  • If the correct replacement blows again, stop. A short, overloaded component, damaged wiring, or module fault may be present.
  • Do not probe or service airbag, ABS, fuel-pump, starter, or high-current circuits without the correct service procedure and tools.
  • For a warning lamp, scan the relevant control module for codes. A fuse check alone cannot clear or diagnose it.
  • The Integrated Power Module is under the hood near the battery. Match the molded cavity code on its cover to the Mopar chart.
